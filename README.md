## My Story

My route into software engineering has been different from the plan I had at the beginning. I wanted to study Computer Science, but completed a Diploma in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Dhaka Polytechnic Institute. That education gave me a technical foundation, while programming remained the field I wanted to pursue.

My interest in coding began when I watched someone build software and realized that programming could turn an idea into a working product. I explored web development independently, then joined the Programming Hero AI Driven Full Stack Web Engineering course to follow a structured path and work with greater consistency.

I have completed the foundations of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, asynchronous JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and Git. I am now learning React and Next.js through practical projects. My goal is to become a full stack engineer who understands both the interface people use and the systems that make a product work.
